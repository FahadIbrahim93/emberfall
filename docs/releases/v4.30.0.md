# EMBERFALL v4.30.0 — "The Hangar"

Released 2026-11-11. Play it at **https://emberfall-game-two.vercel.app/**

The hangar used to be one long shop page. Every upgrade was the obvious buy, weapons offered no choice, and on a phone the Launch button sat four and a half screens down. This release rebuilds it around the docked ship, adds real choices, and keeps the boards fair while doing it.

## What's new

**Five bays.** Ship, Systems, Arms, Style and Yard sit around the docked hull, with a command bar (bays, Back, Test range, Launch) that stays in view on a phone, a tablet or a desktop. The flight sheet shows the stats your current fit actually launches with.

**Look before you buy.** Tap anything you don't own and you see what it changes against your current fit, its price and a "Buy" button. Nothing is spent on a single tap. Equipping something you already own is still one tap.

**Arms.** Four weapon patterns (Spread, Rail, Scatter, Seeker) with two mods each, and three lenses for SERAPH. Each pattern is tuned to the same total output as Spread at every ordnance tier, so they change how you fight, not how hard you hit. "Try in range" lets you fly a pattern before buying it; range runs are never scored.

**Systems.** Six modules, each with a trade-off, a second module slot, a sigil per hull and three loadout presets per hull. Each hull also has frame doctrines: three rows of two choices that open at mastery I, III and V.

**Yard.** Three daily contracts (easy, medium, hard), scaled to how far you usually get, plus a weekly yard order. The standing ladder now continues past Yardmaster.

**Style.** Mastery ranks 6 to 10 add a dock decal, contrail shapes, an ACE hull title, a pad ring and a gold service stripe. If you flew before this release, a Founder plate is waiting for you.

## Fair boards

- New gear never flies on the Daily Gauntlet, Boss Rush, flight school or the range.
- On Endless and the Solar Tour, gear can't push any stat past the strongest setup that was already possible before this release.
- Every scored run now carries a short loadout code, shown on the in-game boards, the stats page and pilot pages. Daily runs read `v:std`. Runs from before this release show no code.

## Fixes

- Four paid perks never worked in flight. Rampart now launches with an extra life, Glasswing launches on one life, Needle bolts pass through the first hostile they kill, and the Mastery 2 dash charge is granted.
- Boss Rush now runs on standard issue as intended. It had been flying with sigils and mastery bonuses.
- Hull stat bars rendered with no height, and the save-code field could never accept a pasted code. Both work now.

## Your save

Existing saves upgrade automatically and keep everything: alloy, hulls, refits, paints, sigils, mastery and honor. Old v3 export codes still import. Saves stay on your device; back them up from the hangar with an export code (the hangar now reminds you after a large purchase).

## Under the hood

- New module `js/hangar.js`. Service worker cache `emberfall-v4.66`.
- Supabase: an additive migration (`standings_fit`) makes the public board readers return the loadout code. It was applied after a full backup; signatures, grants and ranking are unchanged.
- Tests at release: 102 browser, 61 simulation, 47 release, 12 SQL migration and 75 in-game checks, all passing.
