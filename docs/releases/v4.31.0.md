# EMBERFALL v4.31.0

Released 2026-11-14. Play it at **https://emberfall-game-two.vercel.app/**

The menus had grown three generations of buttons, tabs and dialogs. This release gives them one system, rebuilds Settings, and keeps the keyboard inside every dialog.

## What's new

**One UI layer.** A single stylesheet now owns the shell's type, colour, space and shared controls — buttons, tabs, switches, segments and cards — so the title, Settings, pause, the hangar and the boards share one language. Colourblind palettes restyle those menus, not only the playfield.

**Settings rebuilt.** Audio, Display, Controls and Accessibility sit in four labelled sections, each control with a short hint and a live readout on every slider. **Restore defaults** asks first, then resets only the settings; your alloy, unlocks, saves and scores are not touched. Reduce motion is a setting of its own, seeded from your device until you change it.

**Opens where you expect.** Settings used to jump to the bottom, because focus landed on Done. The heading, Restore defaults and Done now stay on screen; you scroll only the section list.

**The keyboard stays in the dialog.** What's new and Pilot data had no focus trap — Tab walked the page behind them. Every dialog is now trapped and closable with Escape. The boon draft and game over stay as decisions you have to make.

**Easier to read.** Informational text is no smaller than 11 px, and switches, segments and tabs meet a 44 px target. The pause action labelled "Leave to hangar" actually returned to the title; it now reads "Leave to bridge".

## Your save

Career saves are unchanged and stay on your device. Back them up from the hangar with an export code. Settings are stored separately; restoring defaults never touches a career.

## Under the hood

- New stylesheet `css/ui.css`, guarded by `tools/ui-audit.js`. Service worker cache `emberfall-v4.68`.
- Hostile-fire drawing and palette colour conversion take less work per frame.
- Playwright 1.65.0 and PGlite 0.5.16 (the latter is a test-only dependency).
- **No database change.** Existing scores, profiles and loadout codes are untouched.

Tests at release: 106 browser, 61 simulation, 47 release and 75 in-game checks, all passing.
